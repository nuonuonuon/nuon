Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

nuon is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Nazar Shpyg. It accesses only the owner's own Google account, with the owner's permission.

What data is accessed

Gmail messages, Google Calendar events and Google Contacts of the owner's account, used to read, summarise and organise the owner's email and schedule.

How it is used and stored

Data is processed on the owner's own computer for the owner's personal use. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
